# HIS Business Role Catalogue

The starting list of HIS business roles (Settings ▸ Users ▸ Business Roles).
A job post points at these roles; each role knows the Odoo groups it grants and, for cashiers, its till.
The data is `noupdate`: edit roles in the UI, upgrades keep your changes.
Beta modules (CRM/admissions, content, Campus+, recruitment) get their roles later.
Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-roles-mdm-design.md`.
